About the Founding Institution

The Techno India Group

Founded 1984 Techno India Group

Techno India Group is one of Eastern India's largest and most respected educational conglomerates, with over four decades of commitment to quality technical education, research and institutional development across West Bengal and Northeast India.

With its flagship Techno India University at the helm, the group spans engineering, management, design, law, and applied sciences — and has now extended its vision to startup incubation through AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council, West Bengal's first Atal Incubation Centre.

What is AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council

About the Atal
Incubation Centre

AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council is formally supported by Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), NITI Aayog — a Government of India flagship programme that establishes world-class incubation centres across India to nurture innovative startups into scalable, sustainable enterprises.

🏛️
Government-Backed

Directly supported by Atal Innovation Mission, NITI Aayog, Government of India — with grant-in-aid of up to ₹10 Crores over five years to build world-class incubation infrastructure.

🎯
Three Focus Sectors

AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council exclusively incubates startups in Social Innovation & Social Business, Emerging Technology (AI, CS-IT, Cybersecurity, EdTech), and Media, Entertainment & Design.

📍
West Bengal's First

AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council will be West Bengal's first government-recognised Atal Incubation Centre — positioning Kolkata and Eastern India as a serious destination for AI, social enterprise and the creative economy.

🚀
What Founders Get

Structured incubation from idea to MVP to market — workspace, AI/Product Lab, Media & Design Studio, expert mentorship, business clinics, investor connects and milestone-driven discipline.

📋
AIM Mandate

As an AIM-recognised AIC, AIC Techno Innovation and Incubation Council operates in full compliance with Atal Innovation Mission guidelines — with transparent selection, published policies, governance and regular public reporting.

Re-Craft Atelier Foundation
"Where hands learn, hearts belong and communities thrive."
Section 8 Non-Profit · Est. 2025 · Kolkata

Re-Craft Atelier Foundation is a circular-economy social enterprise that transforms discarded furniture and textiles into marketable products — building future-ready, green livelihoods for under-served youth, women, differently-abled persons and artisan communities through hands-on craft, skilling and market access. As AIC Techno's Fab Lab partner, Re-Craft brings making, prototyping and sustainable production capability to the ecosystem.

🪑
